The Companion

For reading, journaling and circles

Original Sin Is A Lie: A Companion walks a reader or a small group through the book chapter by chapter, so that people who have left the church but not God have somewhere to do the work instead of only reading about it.

Twenty chapters and an epilogue, each with an anchor, journal prompts, ruled pages and a Take It Further practice. A glossary of the words, people and books. Group agreements, a safety and referral page, and session maps for six, twelve or twenty-one weeks.

“Bob's strategy here isn't to tear down your defense of original sin. He is merely offering his perspective.”Joseph Wilson-Doan, Amazon review
“As an atheist, I would definitely recommend reading this to fellow non-believers, believers and everyone in between!”Vinayak Rao, Goodreads review
