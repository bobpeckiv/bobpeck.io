Privacy

What this site keeps track of.

Last updated November 9, 2026

The short version

This site doesn’t sell anything directly, ask you to log in, or keep a database of visitors. It does use the Meta Pixel, a small piece of code from Meta (the company behind Facebook and Instagram), so I can see which pages people visit and show my work to people who’ve already shown interest in it.

What the Meta Pixel collects

When you load a page here, the pixel can send Meta the page address, the time of the visit, basic browser and device details, and which outbound links you click, like a link to my Etsy shop, Amazon or Stan store. Meta may connect this to a Facebook or Instagram account if you have one. I see this information in aggregate, as counts and audiences, not as a list of names.

How I use it

To understand which pages and products people care about, to measure whether my ads work, and to show ads on Facebook and Instagram to people who have visited this site. Meta’s own use of the data is covered by the Meta Privacy Policy.

Your choices

You can control how Meta uses this kind of data in your Facebook ad settings, opt out of interest-based ads through the Digital Advertising Alliance, or block third-party cookies and trackers in your browser. The site works fine either way.

Other sites I link to

Purchases happen on Etsy, Amazon, Audible and Stan, and some pages embed players from Spotify and YouTube. Each of those has its own privacy policy, and what you do there is between you and them.
